source: sum(rate(jvm_gc_pause_seconds_sum[1m])) by (application) / on(application) system_cpu_count
feasibility: feasible
warning: Counter referenced without rate(); using LAST_OVER_TIME to preserve raw cumulative value
---
TS metrics-*
| WHERE @timestamp >= ?_tstart AND @timestamp <= ?_tend
| WHERE jvm_gc_pause_seconds_sum IS NOT NULL OR system_cpu_count IS NOT NULL
| STATS jvm_gc_pause_seconds_sum_rate_sum = SUM(RATE(jvm_gc_pause_seconds_sum)), system_cpu_count = MAX(LAST_OVER_TIME(system_cpu_count)) BY time_bucket = TBUCKET(5 minute), application
| EVAL computed_value = (jvm_gc_pause_seconds_sum_rate_sum / system_cpu_count)
| KEEP time_bucket, application, computed_value
| SORT time_bucket ASC
