source: sum(irate(node_cpu_seconds_total{instance="$node",job="$job", mode="system"}[5m])) / scalar(count(count(node_cpu_seconds_total{instance="$node",job="$job"}) by (cpu)))
feasibility: feasible
warning: Approximated PromQL arithmetic using same-bucket ES|QL math
warning: Dropped variable-driven label filters during migration
warning: Approximated nested count(count()) as COUNT_DISTINCT(cpu)
warning: PromQL series labels were not retained; output is bucket-level and may collapse multiple source series
---
TS metrics-*
| WHERE @timestamp >= ?_tstart AND @timestamp <= ?_tend
| WHERE node_cpu_seconds_total IS NOT NULL
| STATS node_cpu_seconds_total_mode_system_irate_sum = SUM(IRATE(CASE((mode == "system"), node_cpu_seconds_total, NULL), 5m)), node_cpu_seconds_total_instance_job_count = COUNT_DISTINCT(cpu) BY time_bucket = TBUCKET(5 minute)
| EVAL computed_value = (node_cpu_seconds_total_mode_system_irate_sum / node_cpu_seconds_total_instance_job_count)
| KEEP time_bucket, computed_value
| SORT time_bucket ASC
