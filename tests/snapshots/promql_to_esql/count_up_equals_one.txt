source: count(up == 1)
feasibility: feasible
warning: count() over a comparison collapses matching samples to series by the instance dimension only (the query supplied no grouping labels); if matching series share an instance but differ on another label such as job, the count may be under-stated — add the distinguishing label to by(...) or verify the series identity
---
FROM metrics-*
| WHERE @timestamp >= ?_tstart AND @timestamp <= ?_tend
| WHERE up == 1
| STATS series_present = COUNT(*) BY service.instance.id
| STATS up_count = COUNT(*)
