source: rate(http_requests_total[5m]) or rate(http_errors_total[5m])
feasibility: feasible
warning: PromQL 'or': kept both metrics — left operand takes precedence and the right operand fills series where the left has no data
warning: PromQL series labels were not retained; output is bucket-level and may collapse multiple source series
---
TS metrics-*
| WHERE @timestamp >= ?_tstart AND @timestamp <= ?_tend
| WHERE http_requests_total IS NOT NULL OR http_errors_total IS NOT NULL
| STATS http_requests_total_rate = RATE(http_requests_total), http_errors_total_rate = RATE(http_errors_total) BY time_bucket = TBUCKET(5 minute)
| EVAL computed_value = COALESCE(http_requests_total_rate, http_errors_total_rate)
| KEEP time_bucket, computed_value
| SORT time_bucket ASC
