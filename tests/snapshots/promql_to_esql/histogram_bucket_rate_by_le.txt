source: sum(rate(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket[5m])) by (le)
feasibility: feasible
warning: Source PromQL used rate() but http_request_duration_seconds_bucket is typed as gauge in the target index; rendered as AVG_OVER_TIME instead. Fix the ingest mapping to mark this field as a counter to get a true rate.
---
TS metrics-*
| WHERE @timestamp >= ?_tstart AND @timestamp <= ?_tend
| WHERE http_request_duration_seconds_bucket IS NOT NULL
| STATS http_request_duration_seconds_bucket = SUM(AVG_OVER_TIME(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket, 5m)) BY time_bucket = TBUCKET(5 minute), le
| SORT time_bucket ASC
